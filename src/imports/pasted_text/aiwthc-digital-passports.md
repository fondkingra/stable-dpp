From complex to one scan
How a Digital Product Passport works
We remove the blockchain complexity. You bring the product data — we turn it into a tamper‑proof passport your buyers and regulators can trust in four steps.

STEP 01
📦
Add your product
Upload material composition, origin, certifications and sustainability data — via portal, Excel, or ERP/PLM integration.

STEP 02
⛓️
We anchor it on‑chain
Each passport is written to a Hyperledger blockchain — immutable, verifiable, and impossible to fake.

STEP 03
🏷️
Generate the carrier
A unique QR, NFC or RFID tag is created for every item or batch, GS1 Digital Link‑ready.

STEP 04
📱
One scan reveals all
Buyers, regulators and consumers scan to see the full verified lifecycle — instantly, in any language.
